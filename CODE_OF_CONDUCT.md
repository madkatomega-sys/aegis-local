# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Aegis-Local to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to help, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, especially in code review
- Critiquing code and ideas, not people
- Owning mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion, review, or other project activity
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any chat
channels the project runs) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the contact details on
the maintainer's GitHub profile ([madkatomega-sys](https://github.com/madkatomega-sys)). Reports
are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may respond with a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent
ban, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior. Maintainers who don't follow or enforce
this code in good faith may face the same consequences.
